Introduction

This guide is for my own learning purposes and not intended for public consumption.


With this documentation I aim to provide myself with a quick guide I can refer to when implementing my .css files on sites external to my normal development environment. I will attempt to write tidy and consumable documentation and while I'm at it I may to look to create and constantly improve a CRUD app to link into the rest off my ecosystem.

Breakpoints

I'm trying to keep things simple and work with 5 distinct breakpoints to cover most devices/window sizes responsively. Start with the smallest layout and work upwards, a mobile first design focus. Anything less than 396px or undefined will have 1 column at 100% width.

Name Class prefix Breakpoint
Extra Small none <396px
Small col-sm- ≥396px
Medium col-md- ≥600px
Large col-lg- ≥900px
Extra Large col-xl- ≥1200px
